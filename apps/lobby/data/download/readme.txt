You sit back. City lights bleeding through the glass.  
Just you and the screen now.

You started writing those names down weeks ago.  
Weird ones. Same reflexes. Same aim. Different games.  
Didn’t feel human.

So you kept tracking.

Match after match, lobby after lobby — there they were again.  
Always something off. Always a pattern.  
And that one tag kept showing up: GLHF.

Could’ve been a joke.  
Some dead clan.  
Old forum kids playing pirate.

Still… you couldn’t let it go.

You searched. Not surface-level stuff.  
You dug through pastebins, scraped dumps, dead Discord threads.  
Some archive barely holding together.

And then — that thread.  
Two replies. Both deleted.  
One link still alive.

This login screen.

You’ve got your list — `nicks.txt`. All the names you caught.  
And your favorite wordlist — `rockyou.txt`.  
It’s thin, but maybe enough.

Feels stupid.  
Feels like bait.

But you're already here.

You lean in.  
And type the first name.
